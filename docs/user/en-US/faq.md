# Frequently asked questions

## How to use this page
Find the question, then follow the matching task guide.

## 1. Where is my saved file?
Folder projects write to the selected directory. Browser projects belong to the current site and browser profile. Downloads use the browser download location.

## 2. Why is my project missing in another browser?
Local browser data is not shared across profiles. Export from the original environment and restore in the target.

## 3. Is reloading a backup?
No. Reloading can clear diagnostic history. Use export or a complete folder backup.

## 4. Does a tour recipe contain the model?
No. It stores shots and descriptions. Reopening requires the matching model.

## 5. Can I revoke an HTML file?
No. It contains the complete model. Use protected presentations when revocation is required.

## 6. Does offline playback require sign-in?
The single-file player does not authenticate recipients. Workbench and protected presentations have separate access rules.

## 7. Why does the exported model look different?
The independent player does not copy all workbench environment maps and post effects. Inspect the actual exported file.

## 8. Why is Demo package missing?
Check document capability, workspace, read-only state and deployment restrictions.

## 9. Why can I not import a CAD file?
The required importer may not be included. Confirm format support or use a complete GLB.

## 10. Why are textures missing?
Check embedded resources and resolved references. Offline packages cannot rely on remote resources.

## 11. Why was saving refused?
Check read-only targets, folder permissions, space and conflicts. Do not clear projects to remove an error.

## 12. Can I immediately repeat a timed-out write?
Read back state first. Timeout does not mean it was not executed; repeating a write may conflict.

## 13. Is WebGPU required?
No. WebGL is the default; WebGPU is an optional Beta.

## 14. Does passing environment checks mean certification?
No. Capability probes and product support levels are separate.

## 15. Do I need a browser extension?
Ordinary viewing needs no browser extension. Gateways and XR system components depend on delivery scope.

## 16. Why are web panels absent from screenshots?
Image and scene-video export capture the scene. Tutorials require separate interface recording.

## 17. Why is video silent?
Built-in scene recording does not capture audio.

## 18. Why did I receive WebM?
The browser uses WebM when preferred MP4 encoding is unavailable.

## 19. Why does cancellation still take time?
Encoding already in progress may need to finish to release resources, but no download follows.

## 20. Are diagnostics sent automatically?
No. You preview, download and decide whether to send the file.

## 21. Can clearing caches solve every problem?
No. Back up and diagnose first. Clearing site data may delete local projects.

## 22. Why do Chinese device names remain in English UI?
Device and model names are user content, outside interface translation.

For unresolved issues see [Diagnostics & support](support.md).
